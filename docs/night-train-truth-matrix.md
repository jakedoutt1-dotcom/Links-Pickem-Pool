## Fixed truth before exhibits

Victim: Elias Vane, archivist. Culprit: Mara Holt, passenger and archive broker. Scene: compartment C4 (old berth 3). Fatal act: the unrequested T2 return glass. Means are described non-instructionally; no substance or harmful procedure is supplied. Motive: Vane's original folio F27 would expose Holt's sale of reproduction F72 as the promised original. Method, opportunity, motive and identity are separate propositions.

Holt used a communal navy service coat, reached C4 by the available internal service approach, returned with T2 and removed F27 afterward. Her later luggage deposit, joined acknowledgement and authenticated document correction identify her. A faceless coat reflection does not. No borrowed credential identifies the culprit, no early confession reveals the solution, and five miraculous continuous CCTV alibis do not solve the case for players.

The door is an apparent obstacle, not an impossible mechanical trick. H2 reaches Q1 opposite C4's vestibule, not the victim's table. A visitor still approaches the compartment door. Vane can secure that door after accepting a delivery; finding the bolt intact afterward does not date its closure. No exact lock-defeat method is required. The waxed cord/scuff are scene observations, not proof of secret mechanics.

## Suspect alternatives and resolved red herrings

* Bell delivered T1 and accepted an unauthorized courier gratuity. T1 was cleared before last living contact and tests clean. His occupation and communal coat are not identity evidence for T2.
* Quill's unapproved reproduction repair gives her a professional dispute; it does not identify her as carrier or explain Holt's signed fraudulent transaction. She witnesses scene discovery but does not receive a magical all-evening alibi.
* Salt's catering dispute is real, but the food and first glass test clean; the return event is distinct.
* March's credential oversight explains concern about a security inquiry. The authenticated discovery log is corroborated, and exterior seals exclude the window route without exonerating by motive alone.
* Frost lied to protect a source and forwarded an earlier photograph. Her correction explains a lie; neither a lie nor its explanation is treated as proof of murder or a complete later alibi.
* Holt's photograph claim fails in packet five, but this alone leaves multiple possible people. Packet seven adds positive independent identification and physical connection. Investigators must still cite method and motive.

Final `eliminates` metadata exists only on the final corroborated identity report for engine uniqueness checking. Earlier records never exhaustively clear five people. Public APIs must not leak solution/accepted values before the reveal.
