📜 FOOTBALL SQUARES RULES

How Football Squares works
• The commissioner creates a board and chooses any NFL game on the schedule, including regular season, playoffs, conference championships and the Super Bowl.
• Each board has 100 squares in a 10×10 grid. Players claim available squares before the number draw.
• The row and column numbers stay hidden while squares are being selected. When the commissioner closes the board, Links randomly assigns 0–9 to each team and locks the numbers.
• Winners are based on the last digit of each team's score at the end of the 1st Quarter, Halftime, 3rd Quarter and Final.
• Example: if the away team has 17 and the home team has 24, the winning square is Away 7 / Home 4.
• Links follows the selected NFL game and highlights the winning square as quarter scores become available.
• Once numbers are drawn, square ownership and number positions are locked for that board.

📜 Standard Links March Madness Rules

• Fill out the entire 64-team main bracket before the deadline.
• Opening-round / First Four winners occupy their assigned main-bracket slot; the Links bracket starts with the main Round of 64.
