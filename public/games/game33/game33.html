THE 33 GAME

Up to 32 players. Each player gets one NFL team for the 18-week regular season. Exactly 33 points wins the week; otherwise the rollover continues.

3️⃣3️⃣ How Game 33 Works

• Maximum 32 players — one player per NFL team.
• Each player keeps the same assigned NFL team for all 18 regular-season weeks.
• Exactly 33 points, win or lose, wins that week.
• No 33 means the rollover continues.
• Multiple eligible 33s are all shown as winners.
