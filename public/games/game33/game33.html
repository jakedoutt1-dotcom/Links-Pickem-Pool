3️⃣3️⃣ GAME 33 RULES

32 entries maximum. Each entry is assigned one unique NFL team for the regular season.

FINAL SCORE ONLY. A team must finish its NFL game with exactly 33 points. Reaching 33 during a live game does not count.

The NFL result does not matter: the assigned team can win or lose. If two or more eligible teams finish with exactly 33, they are tied Game 33 winners and split that week's prize. If nobody finishes with 33, the prize rolls over.

Teams stay assigned for all 18 regular-season weeks in this LINKS pool. The commissioner controls assignments and player status.
