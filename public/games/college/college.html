College Pick'em Rules

The commissioner chooses the college games that count each week from games involving the current Top 25. Pick the winner of each selected matchup before the weekly lock. Correct picks earn one win. The tiebreaker is the combined score of the final selected game. Compare Picks unlocks after picks lock.

LINKS keeps the existing straight-up format. Confidence points and point spreads are not enabled unless the commissioner format is expanded later.
