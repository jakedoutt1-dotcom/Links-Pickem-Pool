Where does our information appear?

Active venue information is shared with the existing location-based offers, supported pool-page branding and game venue displays. Hosted Trivia uses the host’s location for its venue banner. Guests must allow location access for GPS matching; uncertain matches may need venue confirmation. Updating this page changes the saved venue record; open pages pick up changes on their next location check or reload.

Your account does not provide access to LINKS Admin, other venues, or their private information. Use your account’s password recovery if you cannot sign in.
