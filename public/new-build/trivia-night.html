    How to cast

    For wireless casting, put both devices on the same Wi-Fi. Venue guest Wi-Fi may block device discovery. A laptop with HDMI is the most dependable option.

    iPhone / iPad · AirPlay

    On a separate iPhone or iPad, open the TV display. Open Control Center → Screen Mirroring → choose your AirPlay-compatible TV. Enter the TV’s code if asked. Turn this display device sideways for a wider picture.

    Apple’s AirPlay instructions

    Android · Screen Cast

    Open the TV display on a separate Android device. In Google Home, select your compatible TV or Chromecast and use Cast my screen. Some phones also have Cast or Smart View in Quick Settings. Names and support vary by device.

    Google’s Android casting help

    Computer · Chrome or HDMI

    Open the TV display in Chrome and use the browser’s Cast option to send that tab to a compatible TV. Or connect the computer with HDMI, choose the TV’s HDMI input, and open the display full screen.

    Mirroring shows everything on that device, including notifications. Casting your host phone also shows its controls. Use a separate display device so you can keep hosting privately on your phone. LINKS cannot start AirPlay or screen mirroring for you.
