How to survive the broadcast

Everyone answers timed trivia. A wrong or missing answer puts you at risk: fail the next challenge and lose a life. Everyone plays every challenge. Lose all three lives and become an Echo; clearing two challenges restores one life.

Correct trivia earns 100–150 points; a cleared challenge earns 150. Twelve rounds lead to the Final Broadcast. Your score grants up to three starting transmitter bars, plus one if you still have a life. Each correct final answer adds two bars. First to reach 12 wins; equal leading progress shares the win. After eight final questions, the furthest signal wins if nobody reaches 12.

Scan the invitation to join with a name. No account needed. For a shared TV, open the private TV display on a computer or TV browser, then use full screen. You can also mirror that browser. Use sound on the TV only to avoid an echo; each phone has its own controls. No one needs to host questions.

Suspense and mild spooky imagery, no gore. Fresh API trivia with backup questions; puzzles change each game. Frequency Lock clues arrive five seconds apart. Enable Spooky voice on one device to hear narration and each new clue; available voices vary by device. Questions may repeat between games. Rooms expire after two hours.
