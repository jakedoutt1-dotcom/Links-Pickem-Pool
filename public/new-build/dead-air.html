How to survive the broadcast

Everyone answers timed trivia. A wrong or missing answer puts you at risk: fail the next challenge and lose a life. Everyone plays every challenge. If everyone is correct and nobody needs an Echo comeback, the studio awards a 150-point clean-broadcast bonus instead and moves straight on. Lose all three lives and become an Echo; clearing two challenges restores one life.

Correct trivia earns 100–150 points; a cleared challenge earns 150. Twelve broadcasts lead to the Final Broadcast. You can change a saved answer until the server timer expires; changing it also resets its speed-bonus time. The room creator can pause and resume the broadcast. Your score grants up to three starting transmitter bars, plus one if you still have a life. Each correct final answer adds two bars. The leading signal wins when anyone reaches 12 bars, or after eight final questions. Total points break equal progress; equal progress and points share the win.
