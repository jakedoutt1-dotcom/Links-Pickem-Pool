    How to play

    Bank shots off the stone walls to reach the cup. Touch the ball and pull back to aim and set power, then release to shoot. Drag the surrounding course to rotate the camera. Use Behind ball to reset the view; the + and − buttons or mouse wheel zoom. Dots show a short preview of your shot, including rebounds. A tap does not shoot. Use the aiming pad and Putt button if you prefer. Full screen expands the game; Exit full screen returns to the page. Keyboard: focus the course, use left and right arrows to aim, up and down to set power, and Space to putt. Optional Button controls also include an aiming pad and Putt button. Higher power rolls farther; the ball must reach the cup slowly enough to drop. Near the cup, power switches to a gentler putting range and the flag lifts out. Fewest strokes wins. Play solo or share a room with up to eight players. Everyone plays independently, then the scoreboard appears once all players finish. Ten strokes maximum. The host can end a round and mark unfinished players as Did not finish. Equal scores tie. Airborne shots on Hidden Bend are affected by its crosswind; ground putts stay predictable. Rounds cycle through the available holes; rooms expire after 24 hours. Later holes have moving gates, water and narrow ramps. Water adds one penalty stroke (up to the 10-stroke limit) and returns you to your previous shot position. Explore different angles for riskier routes, or use the safer dry path. Course signs show LINKS branding and sample advertising placements.
