    Drag across the course to aim, or use the slider. Set power, then putt.

    How to play

    Bank shots off the stone walls to reach the cup. Drag sideways to aim or use the Aim slider. Higher power rolls farther; the ball must reach the cup slowly enough to drop. Near the cup, power switches to a gentler putting range and the flag lifts out. Fewest strokes wins. Play solo or share a room with up to eight players. Everyone plays independently, then the scoreboard appears once all players finish. Ten strokes maximum. The host can end a round and mark unfinished players as Did not finish. Equal scores tie. For now, each round replays this hole; rooms expire after 24 hours. Course signs show LINKS branding and sample advertising placements.
