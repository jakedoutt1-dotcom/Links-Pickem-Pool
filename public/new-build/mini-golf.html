    How to play

    Bank shots off the stone walls to reach the cup. Drag to aim on the course or the aiming pad. Keyboard: use left and right arrows on the pad. Higher power rolls farther; the ball must reach the cup slowly enough to drop. Near the cup, power switches to a gentler putting range and the flag lifts out. Fewest strokes wins. Play solo or share a room with up to eight players. Everyone plays independently, then the scoreboard appears once all players finish. Ten strokes maximum. The host can end a round and mark unfinished players as Did not finish. Equal scores tie. Rounds cycle through three holes; rooms expire after 24 hours. Later holes have moving gates, water and narrow ramps. Water adds one penalty stroke (up to the 10-stroke limit) and returns you to your previous shot position. Explore different angles for riskier routes, or use the safer dry path. Course signs show LINKS branding and sample advertising placements.
