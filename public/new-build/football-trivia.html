How to play & fairness

Choose Easy (10 base points), Medium (20), or Hard (40). Correct answers also earn a speed bonus: up to 5 on Easy, 10 on Medium, or 20 on Hard. The bonus is the maximum multiplied by the fraction of time remaining, rounded down. Faster answers earn more; wrong or expired answers earn zero. Ranked bonuses use the server clock. One scored attempt per verified connected account per week, across all pools and difficulties. Easy allows 6 seconds per question, Medium 8 seconds, and Hard 10 seconds, enforced by the server. Correct answers gain 10 yards and reset downs; four consecutive misses end the drive. Eight correct answers reach the end zone. Touchdowns are a badge, not bonus points; you may finish all ten plays. Wrong or timed-out answers earn zero. Starting consumes your weekly attempt. Refreshing resumes it, but does not reset the current clock. Network delays can use clock time. Your chosen name appears on the signed-in LINKS trivia leaderboard.

Questions use NFL rules and history, with source links shown after answers. This first bank has 36 questions; repeat questions across weeks are possible. Short timers discourage lookups but cannot guarantee cheat-free play. Practice is unlimited, has sample questions, and never affects rankings or pool scores.
