HOW TO PLAY · CONFIDENCE

How the pool works

Pick the winner of each active matchup, then assign confidence points according to the pool rules—the stronger your confidence, the higher the point value. Save the full card before the deadline. Games lock at their scheduled times, results award points after completion, and standings rank players by earned confidence points. The commissioner controls the slate and pool settings.
