How to play

Choose available squares and press Save squares before kickoff and before the number draw. Unsaved selections are not reserved. Saved squares cannot be changed or released. Your commissioner draws unique digits 0–9 for each axis. The last digit of each team’s cumulative score determines the Q1, halftime, Q3 and final winners. Overtime counts in the final.
