How to play

Choose available squares and press Save squares before kickoff and before the number draw. Unsaved selections are not reserved. Saved squares cannot be changed or released. Numbers draw automatically when every square is claimed, or at kickoff, whichever comes first. Payments do not delay the draw. Once drawn, selections are locked. LINKS draws unique digits 0 through 9 for each axis. The commissioner only needs to mark payments. The last digit of each team’s cumulative score determines the Q1, halftime, Q3 and final winners. Choose a 25-, 50- or 100-square board. On a 50-square board each home-team row has two digits; on a 25-square board both rows and columns have two. Either displayed digit matches. Overtime counts in the final.
