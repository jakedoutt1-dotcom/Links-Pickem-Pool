HOW TO PLAY · GAME 33

How the pool works

Each active player is assigned an NFL team for the season/yearly draw. Follow your assigned team’s final score each week. A final score of exactly 33 wins; if more than one player hits 33, the result is a tie. If nobody hits 33, the game rolls to the next week. The commissioner manages players, Active/Pending status, team assignments, the draw, weekly finalization and rollover.
