HOW TO PLAY · MARCH MADNESS

How the pool works

Join the pool, fill out your tournament bracket by advancing teams through each round, and save it before the bracket deadline. Once games begin, locked selections cannot be changed. LINKS advances results through the bracket and updates standings as tournament games finish. The commissioner manages pool access, rules and tournament settings.

TRY THE DEMO BELOW

Want to see how this game works before joining a pool? Scroll down and try the practice game. Make selections, use the controls, and learn the flow. Demo picks do not affect a real pool.

USING LINKS PLAYMAKER

Playmaker is optional and separate from your pool entry. When an eligible selection supports Playmaker, save your pool picks first, then choose ADD TO PLAYMAKER. LINKS can carry that selection to your Playmaker card, show a verified live moneyline when the odds feed provides one, compare available lines, and let you remove or combine selections before saving the card.
