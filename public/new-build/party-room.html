How do we put it on the TV?
  1. Create your game on LINKS and invite your friends.
  2. Open its TV display or Big screen option, when available.
  3. For the simplest setup, connect a laptop to the TV with HDMI. You can also mirror its browser using AirPlay or Cast if supported.
  4. Keep the shared display on the TV. Each player uses their own phone to answer or play.

Enable sound on one device so the whole room can hear. Available display options vary by game.
