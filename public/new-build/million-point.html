How to play

Reach one million game points by answering 15 questions. Questions progress through easy, medium, and hard. You have 60 seconds per question. Correct answers at levels 5 and 10 guarantee 1,000 and 32,000 points. A wrong answer or timeout ends your run at your last guarantee. Bank your current points before submitting an answer to leave the ladder safely.

Hot Seat: Friends take turns. Each player gets 50/50, Ask the Room (15-second real player vote), and Swap Question once per turn. The creator advances to the next player. There is no automatic countdown between questions—choose to continue or bank.

Head-to-Head: Exactly two players get the same questions. Each has a private 50/50. Answers reveal when the shared clock expires, then the next question starts automatically. A player who banks or misses stays to watch. Highest points wins; equal scores share the win.

Game points only. No cash prizes. No account needed. Rooms expire after 24 hours. Enable sound on one device.
