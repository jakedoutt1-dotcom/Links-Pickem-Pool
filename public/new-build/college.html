HOW TO PLAY · COLLEGE PICK’EM

How the pool works

Join or log in to your pool. The commissioner chooses the college games that count for that week. In Commissioner Admin, they can load the current AP Top 25, set how many games the pool will use, select the ranked matchups themselves, or use RANDOM DRAW to choose from eligible AP Top 25 matchups. The commissioner can reroll or adjust the slate before saving it. Players then pick the team they think will win each active matchup and save the required card before the deadline. Unlocked games can be changed until their lock time. After lock, follow scores, Compare Picks and standings.

TRY THE DEMO BELOW

Want to see how this game works before joining a pool? Scroll down and try the practice game. Make selections, use the controls, and learn the flow. Demo picks do not affect a real pool.
