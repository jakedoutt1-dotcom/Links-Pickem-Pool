HOW TO PLAY LINKS

PICK A GAME. JOIN YOUR PEOPLE. MAKE YOUR PICKS.

LINKS brings different pool games together in one place. Commissioners create pools and choose the games they want. Players join a pool, open an available game, make their selections before the deadline, then follow results and standings.

CREATE A POOLJOIN / POOL LOGIN
1 · CREATE OR JOIN

Create a pool as commissioner, or use Pool Login to join the pool you were invited to.

2 · CHOOSE A GAME

The commissioner selects which LINKS games are available. Players only see the games active for their pool.

3 · MAKE YOUR PICKS

Open a game, make your selections and save them. Each game explains its own scoring and lock rules.

4 · LOCKS & DEADLINES

Picks lock according to that game’s rules. NFL Pick’em, for example, locks each matchup at kickoff.

5 · FOLLOW RESULTS

Use LINKS LIVE, game results and Compare Picks where available to follow the action.

6 · STANDINGS

After results are final, standings show how everyone did according to that pool game’s scoring rules.
