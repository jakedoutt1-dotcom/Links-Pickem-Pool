HOW TO PLAY · CUSTOM POOL

How the pool works

Custom pools use rules created by the commissioner. Join or log in, read the pool rules first, make every required selection, and save before the configured deadline. Locked selections cannot be changed after their deadline. Results and standings follow the commissioner’s scoring setup; the commissioner controls questions/selections, access, deadlines and rules.

Want to see how this game works before joining a pool? Scroll down and try the practice game. Make selections, use the controls, and learn the flow. Demo picks do not affect a real pool.
