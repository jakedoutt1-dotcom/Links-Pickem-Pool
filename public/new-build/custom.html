HOW TO PLAY · INTERACTIVE GUIDE

Learn this game before joining a pool

1. Choose your picks. 2. Tap ADD TO PLAYMAKER on any selection you may want on a Playmaker card. 3. Review the live moneyline when available. 4. Save your picks before the lock time. 5. Use the week selector for the next slate. 6. After games lock, follow Results, Compare Picks and Standings.

Playmaker selections are optional and separate from your pool picks. You can combine eligible selections into one card in LINKS Playmaker.
