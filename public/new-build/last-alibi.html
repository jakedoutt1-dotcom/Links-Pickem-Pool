How this works

Choose whether you are playing alone or with friends, enter your name and create the room. If you invite friends, share the room link or QR code and start when everyone is ready.

Inspect named records, compare documents and photographs, and keep private notes. In case-file investigations, discover the current objective and submit your deduction with supporting records to unlock the next packet. Hints are optional.

When the investigation reaches its conclusion, submit who was responsible, where and how, backed by evidence. Each player keeps their final answer private until the reveal. The optional TV shows only shared story and evidence.
