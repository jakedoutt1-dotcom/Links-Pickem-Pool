Controls & rules

Phone: drag the run pad; tap Pass A or B to throw, then steer the receiver after the catch. Hold Sprint for a short burst. Computer: WASD or arrow keys, 1 / 2 to pass, Space to sprint, P to pause.

Each snap gives you three seconds to get ready before defenders move and the clock starts. One forward pass per down. You get four downs to reach the goal line; there are no first downs in this arcade mode. Each touchdown starts a harder drive. The clock runs during plays only. Your device keeps your best score. After a finished run, enter your public name and save it to the Goal Line scoreboard. Solo runs rank by points and do not earn multiplayer wins.
