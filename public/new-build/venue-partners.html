YOUR VENUE. YOUR UPDATES.

Set it up.
Make it yours.

You upload your own logo and control what your venue ad displays: your promotion text, website link and whether the ad is active. Make changes yourself from your venue dashboard.

01

Create your account

Use your email, verify it and agree to display LINKS flyers at your venue.

02

Add your venue

Upload one logo, enter your address and set your location while at the venue. Recommended logo: 1200 × 600 transparent PNG.

03

Publish your promotions

Choose the logo, link and offer guests see in your venue ad. Turn your venue display on or off. Guests who allow location can see your nearby promotions automatically in supported LINKS placements.

Location permission is required for automatic matching. Indoor GPS can be uncertain; guests may need to confirm the venue. Your logo scales to fit supported placements without stretching or cropping.
