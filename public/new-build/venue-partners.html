SEE HOW IT PLAYS

Your TV. Their phones. Everyone in the game.

No paper. No collecting answers.

Run Hosted Trivia from your phone. Guests answer on their own phones, or use one phone per team. Nobody needs to write answers down or bring answer sheets to the host. LINKS records submissions and calculates the scores.

Answer correctly and submit faster to earn more points. Correct answers earn base points plus a speed bonus based on when you press Submit answer. A wrong answer earns zero. Once submitted, an answer is locked.

Run your first trivia night

  1. Arrange host access. Contact Jake or use Hosted Trivia host options to arrange your paid pass.
  2. Create your room. Choose individual or team play, categories and difficulty. Use trivia-library questions or your own prepared questions.
  3. Select your venue and connect the TV. Confirm the GPS match or choose your venue, then open Connect a TV. Your venue logo appears on the waiting and question screens.
  4. Invite the crowd. Show the room’s QR code or share its invitation. Guests pick a player or team name; one phone per player or team.
  5. Start the show. Choose the category and start the question. A 10-second countdown gets everyone ready. Players choose an answer and submit to lock it in; faster correct submissions earn a speed bonus.
  6. Reveal and repeat. Reveal the answer when time is up. Guests see their standings on their phones while you control the next question and breaks.

From your TV to their phones.

Your phone runs the night. Host controls and player answers work on phones; the shared TV lets the whole venue read along. There are no paper answer sheets to hand out, collect or score.

Share the other multiplayer games straight from your phone. Create a room and use its Share Invite button to send the invitation through your phone’s sharing options, or let friends scan the room’s QR code. They open the link and join on their own phones. Solo games can be played on your own.

Guests scan the room’s QR code or open a shared invitation. For venue matching, the host can allow location access; GPS looks for a nearby registered partner. If indoor location is unavailable, the host can select the venue. The TV uses the room’s selected venue, so the TV itself does not need GPS.

  1. Choose your format. Contact Jake to confirm the game, host and venue access. Setup can be coordinated remotely.
  2. Invite your guests. Share the game invitation or display its join QR code.
  3. Open the shared display. Where available, use TV display / Big screen on a laptop connected by HDMI, or supported casting. Players keep their phones upright.
  4. Run the night. For hosted trivia, control questions and reveal answers. Guests follow their standings on their phones.
