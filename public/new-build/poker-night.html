    How to play

    Texas Hold’em · No limit · Virtual chips only

    1. Two cards, just for you. Everyone receives two private cards. Make the best five-card poker hand using any combination of your cards and the five community cards.
    2. Blinds start the pot. The two seats after the dealer post the small and big blinds. The dealer button moves each hand.
    3. Four betting rounds. Bet before the flop, after three community cards (the flop), after the fourth (the turn), and after the fifth (the river).
    4. When it’s your turn: check if nothing is owed, call to match, raise to increase the bet, or fold. “Raise to” is your total bet for that round.
    5. Best hand wins. If everyone else folds, the last player wins. At showdown, the best five-card hand wins. Equal hands split the pot; side pots keep all-ins fair.
    6. Keep things moving. If your timer runs out, the game checks when allowed or folds. Refreshing reconnects you to your saved seat on this browser.
    7. One continuous game. Each hand deals automatically after the result countdown. Your chips carry over; folding or losing one hand does not end your game. Play until your stack is gone or you win all the chips. Hosted tournaments increase blinds and balance tables between hands.
    8. Buy back in. If the host allows it, eliminated players can restore their starting virtual chips before the cutoff. Your room shows the limit. There are no payments or payouts.

    Heads-up: the dealer posts the small blind and acts first before the flop. The big blind acts first after the flop.
