HOW TO PLAY · NFL PICK’EM

How the pool works

Join or log in to your pool. Each week, pick the team you think will win every active NFL matchup. Save the complete card to your pool before kickoff. You may unlock and change any game that has not kicked off; each matchup locks automatically at kickoff. After games lock, use Compare Picks, follow LINKS LIVE scores, and check weekly and season standings. The commissioner controls pool access, active weeks, game settings and pool rules.
