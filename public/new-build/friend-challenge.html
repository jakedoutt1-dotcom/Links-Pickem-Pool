    How to play · Rules and deadlines

    Play Solo starts immediately and advances automatically. Beat your personal best, saved on this device separately for each player name, category and difficulty. Solo runs do not count as multiplayer wins. Use Play Again for another run. Play with friends still supports 2–8 players.

    Everyone answers the same ten questions. Easy allows 10 seconds and awards 500 base points; Medium 15 seconds and 1,000; Hard 20 seconds and 1,500. Correct answers earn up to 500 extra points based on time remaining when the server receives them. Wrong, missing or late answers earn zero. Submitted answers cannot be changed.

    Tied leaders continue with extra questions until one leads or the room’s question deck runs out; a remaining tie shares the win. Answers reveal automatically after each timer. Rematches require everyone to be ready.
